// faq

Technical questions

Why do you need the native file and not a printout or scan?

Printing or scanning a file discards its metadata, revision layers, fonts, and signature data. Re-saving or forwarding a file can overwrite them. We send preservation instructions before you submit.

Can you recover content that was deleted?

Only when the file still holds it, as with incremental PDF updates, orphaned objects, or legacy Office fast-save data. We report what is recoverable and what is not.

Does a valid digital signature mean the document is authentic?

No. A valid signature shows that the signed byte range has not changed. We also check what falls outside that range, the certificate chain, and the signing time.

Do you use AI services on client files?

No. Files are processed on closed systems and are not sent to outside AI services.

How long does an examination take?

[Standard turnaround to confirm.] Productions and multi-file correlation are scoped individually, and expedited service is available.

Are findings admissible?

Reports are written to support an evidentiary foundation, and testimony is available. Admissibility requirements vary by jurisdiction.
