Forensic Examination of Digital Documents

The critical evidence may be in the digital documents.

Forensic Digital Document Group examines PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets, images, email, and video to establish when, how, and with what a document was made, and whether it has been changed since. Our findings are written for attorneys, investigators, and the courts.

Core Services

A complete examination of the digital record

Each engagement is scoped to your questions. These are the examinations we perform, alone or in combination.

I.

Deep Metadata Extraction & Analysis

We recover the hidden information stored inside a file, well beyond what “File › Properties” shows, and interpret what it says about the document’s history.

II.

Software & Version Identification

We identify the programs, and their versions, that created the document or worked on it later.

III.

Revision History & Recovery

We trace a file’s revisions and, where the file still holds them, recover deleted or replaced content.

IV.

Digital Signature & Cryptographic Analysis

We verify digital signatures, examine the certificates behind them, and determine whether anything changed after signing.

V.

Font Analysis & Date Consistency

We check whether the fonts embedded in a digital document existed on the date the document claims.

VI.

Date & Timestamp Analysis

We compare the dates in the metadata, the dates printed on the page, and the timestamps recorded deep in the file’s data.

VII.

Composition & Structure Analysis

When one file is assembled from many, such as a declaration with its exhibits, we examine each part on its own, because each can carry its own history.

VIII.

Embedded Object, Image & Duplicate Asset Analysis

We examine images and objects inside a document and identify where the same asset appears elsewhere.

IX.

Scanned Document & OCR Analysis

We examine how a scan was produced and whether its searchable text matches the page image.

X.

Email Header, Routing & Attachment Analysis

We trace an email’s path between servers, check its sending details, and examine its attachments.

XI.

Case-Wide Correlation & Duplicate Detection

We compare documents across one or more productions to find duplicates, shared origins, and inconsistencies.

XII.

Byte-Level File Examination

We examine the raw structure of the file itself to find traces that ordinary tools never display.

XIII.

Specialized File Types

Ask a Question
What types of files can you examine?

All digital file types, including PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, email, and video. If you are unsure about a format, tell us what you have.

Do I need to send the original file?

Yes, wherever possible. Printing, scanning, re-saving, or forwarding a file can change or remove the information we examine. We send preservation instructions before you submit anything.

How long does an examination take?

[Standard turnaround to confirm.] Larger or more complex matters take longer, and expedited service is available.

Are your findings admissible in court?

We work with your legal team to lay the evidentiary foundation for our findings. Admissibility requirements vary by jurisdiction.

How is my data kept confidential?

Your files stay on our closed systems and are not sent to AI services. Files are securely destroyed after the engagement ends.
