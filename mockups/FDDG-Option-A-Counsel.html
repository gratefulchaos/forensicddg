Our Services

What we examine

Paper documents on the left, digital files on the right. Start with the side that matches what you have.

Paper

Forensic Document Examination

Nondestructive laboratory examination of original paper documents.

I.

Handwriting & signature examination

Whether a questioned signature or handwriting was written by the person it is attributed to.

Including guided-hand and deathbed signatures, and holographic wills.
II.

Altered & fabricated documents

Added entries, changed figures, substituted pages, and documents assembled to look genuine.

III.

Ink & paper examination

Infrared and ultraviolet examination to tell different inks and papers apart on the same document.

IV.

Indented writing

Recovering the impressions left by writing on a page that was above this one.

V.

Typewriters, printers & copiers

The machine or process behind typed, printed, and copied pages, including e-mail printouts.

VI.

Writing instruments & line sequence

Which pen made a mark, and which of two crossing strokes was written first.

Lab equipment: Video Spectral Comparator (VSC®80i), ESDA, and 4K stereo microscopy.
Digital

Digital Document Forensics

Deep analysis of the file itself, well beyond what a document viewer shows.

I.

Deep metadata & software identification

The hidden information inside a file, and the programs and versions that created or edited it.

II.

Date, timestamp & font consistency

Dates in the metadata, on the page, and in the file’s binary data, plus whether its fonts existed on the stated date.

III.

Revision history & recovery

Earlier versions of the file, and deleted or superseded content it still holds.

IV.

Digital signatures & cryptographic analysis

Whether an e-signature is valid, and whether the document changed after it was signed.

V.

Composition, structure & embedded assets

Compound files such as a declaration with exhibits, each with its own history; embedded images and duplicate assets; byte-level structure.

VI.

Scans, email & whole productions

How a scan was made and whether its OCR text matches; email headers, routing, and attachments; duplicates and correlations across productions.

All digital file types: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, video, email, and more.

Ask a Question
Is my question a paper one or a digital one?

If you have the physical document (a signed original, a handwritten will, a check), it’s a paper examination. If you have the file itself (a PDF, a Word document, an email), it’s a digital one. Not sure? Tell us what you have and we’ll point you to the right examination.

Can you work from a copy instead of the original?

Handwriting can sometimes be compared from a good copy. Ink, paper, and indented-writing tests need the original. For digital files, we need the native file, not a printout or scan.

Do you work directly with individuals?

[Confirm policy.] We work with attorneys and licensed investigators. If your attorney has asked you to find a document examiner, please call us first.

How long does an examination take?

[Standard turnaround to confirm.] It depends on the number of documents and the examinations needed. Expedited service is available.

Can your examiners testify?

Our examiners can be made available for expert testimony, and for written reports, declarations, or affidavits. Each is arranged separately once we’ve reviewed the matter. Admissibility depends on the court and jurisdiction.
